## Exercise #001 — Kiffa, Mauritania

### Challenge
A photo shared on Twitter. Identify the exact coordinates of where the photo was taken.

### Tools Used
- Visual Analysis
- Shadow Analysis
- Google Earth Historical Imagery
- ExifTool (metadata verification)

### Solution
The photo was taken at the southern edge of Kiffa, Mauritania.

### Coordinates
16°36'33.9"N, 11°23'52.0"W

### Methodology
1. Analyzed visual clues: road type, architecture, vegetation.
2. Used shadow direction to determine camera heading (south).
3. Matched satellite imagery of Kiffa's southern road.
4. Verified structures, utility poles, and terrain.

## Exercise #002 — Flinders Street Station, Melbourne

### Challenge
A photo of a train station shared on social media.  
Find the station name and the tallest structure in the photo.

### Tools Used
- Google Lens
- Google Dorking
- Google Maps

### Solution
- Station: Flinders Street Station
- Tallest Structure: Focus Building (166–167m)

### Coordinates
37.8183° S, 144.9670° E

### Methodology
1. Recognized the iconic yellow facade and domes.
2. Used Google Lens to identify the station.
3. Searched for the tallest building visible in the background.

## Exercise #003 — Presidential Complex, Ankara

### Challenge
Photo of Somali President Farmaajo shaking hands with Turkish President Erdoğan.  
Find the location and coordinates.

### Tools Used
- Google Dorking
- News agencies (AllAfrica, Getty Images, Alamy)
- Wikipedia
- Google Maps

### Solution
The meeting took place at the Presidential Complex in Ankara, Turkey.

### Coordinates
39.9308° N, 32.7989° E

### Methodology
1. Searched for the event: Farmaajo Erdogan April 2017.
2. Confirmed location from news images.
3. Extracted official coordinates from Wikipedia.
4. Verified address: Beştepe, Cumhurbaşkanlığı Blv, Ankara.
